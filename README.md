# Hotel Booking & Revenue Risk Analysis

## Project Status
In Progress

## Project Overview

This project investigates hotel reservation patterns, booking cancellations, seasonal demand, and potential revenue risk using a publicly available hotel booking dataset.

The objective is to identify factors associated with reservation cancellations and develop evidence-based recommendations to improve operational efficiency and revenue management.

## Business Objectives

- Identify seasonal booking trends.
- Determine factors associated with reservation cancellations.
- Evaluate customer segments and booking channels.
- Investigate the relationship between booking lead time and cancellations.
- Estimate potential revenue at risk.
- Develop actionable recommendations for hotel management.
